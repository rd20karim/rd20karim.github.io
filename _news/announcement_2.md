Notification Paper Accepted at CIKM2026!

---

[1082: GeoBenchLLM: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs on Geo-Related Tasks](https://aaai.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Main-track-poster-presentations-1.pdf)

We are pleased to inform you that your submission to the CIKM 2026 Resource Paper Track 82: GeoBenchLLM: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating LLMs on Geo-Related Tasks s been accepted for presentation at the conference and inclusion in the conference proceedings. Congratulations! is year we received 128 resource paper submissions and accepted 46 (~36%) of them as *resource* papers. For all papers, we assigned at least three PC members and one SPC member to provide first-level reviews.
